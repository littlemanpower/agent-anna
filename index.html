FAQ // common questions

Short answers about Agent Anna, the crew, and Cool Hot Sauce. Open a question to read more.

What is Agent Anna?
Agent Anna is your Cool Hot Sauce guide on this page. Chat with her and her crew any time about the sauce, streams, or music.
Who are the three agents?
Anna is the Cool Hot Sauce guide. Mr. Preach X is the founder of Cool Hot Sauce, a gamer and streamer at @LittleManPower. Martin Strayd is a musician and teammate. Choose any of them in Crew & Chat.
Which flavours are there?
Three 100ml bottles, all made in Thailand: Smoked Black, Green Jalapeño and Red Thai Chili. See them in The Sauces, or ask Anna to help you pick one.
Where can I buy Cool Hot Sauce?
On the official Lazada shop. Every flavour card on this page links there too. Check the shop for current prices, postage and stock.
Can I type and use voice at the same time?
No. Typing and voice are one at a time. While a voice chat is on, the page says “Voice chat on - end voice to type.” Hang up voice to type again.
Are you around 24/7?
Yes. Talk with Anna, Mr. Preach X, and Martin Strayd any time.
What is Cool Hot Sauce?
World Famous Cool Hot Sauce is a Thailand National Hot Sauce Brand, built by Mr. Preach X. Just chili, salt and vinegar. No sugar added. No artificial preservatives. The three flavours are Smoked Black, Green Jalapeño and Red Thai Chili. Shop at CoolHotSauce.com or the Lazada shop.
How do I leave Anna a message?
Use Leave a message on this page. The note is saved so nothing gets lost, and Anna will pass it on. You will see “Got it - Anna will pass this on.” Chat is still live if you would rather talk now.
How do you handle privacy, and what does Forget me do?
Anna’s opening line may use the time of day and, if available, an approximate city and weather from your IP. Location is approximate, from IP, used only to show the weather, and not stored. A short visit note can stay in this browser only. Forget me clears that note on this device. Chat you type is sent so Anna can reply. Notes left in the message form are saved for the team and are not readable from the browser.
How does Share on X work?
Share on X opens your own X compose window with a prefilled line about Agent Anna and Cool Hot Sauce. Nothing is posted by this site.
